## Question

What ordered milestones take the local fork from where the **base** stands, as measured, to the **offline finish line**? For each milestone: which in-scope **gaps** it closes, what it proves, and how you can tell it has been reached (an observable check on this PC, scripted with upstream's remote input where possible).

Also decide:

- **How the story gets covered past the Prologue:** a level-jump hook (upstream's `LBP3_BOOT_LEVEL` idea), save files, scripted runs, or human playthroughs.
- **Where the fork's code lives and how it tracks upstream:** for example, merge upstream's history into `lbp3-pc` with an `upstream` remote, or keep the map in a separate planning repo.
- **Where GPU risk sits on the ladder:** the Thruster freeze, the sky colour and the hitches all live in the video core.

The first milestone has to be defined well enough that a build session could start on it tomorrow.

## Answer

Settled on 2026-11-10 with the agent's recommendations, as the map's standing preference allows. The draft of this answer sat among the comments above and was revised after tickets 07, 09 and 10 closed with the human's sessions on the 240 Hz display.

**Decisions**

- **Story coverage past the Prologue:** human playthroughs, one world per session, with a save snapshot kept after each world.
  - After each session the agent copies `user\savedata\CUSA00473` to a dated folder. Restoring a snapshot and walking the Pod menus by remote input reruns any later level without replaying the story. The test scripts keep the human's current saves aside and put them back on exit (`tools/saves.sh`).
